# Stormglass

A 3D hero shooter you play in the browser. Open the game at **https://nefarious-trickster.github.io/stormglass/** — no sign-in needed.

**Play with friends:** choose *Host a match*, send your friend the link and the 4-letter code, and they choose *Join*. Players connect directly to each other; free public MQTT brokers are only used to find one another (and to relay a player who cannot connect directly).
